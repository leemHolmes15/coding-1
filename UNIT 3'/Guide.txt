This  game is a basketball where you shot over a player to win the game in 30 second. 
The challenge is you trying to shot the ball before the timer hits Zero and being defend by a defender .

The game will have a welcome screen saying the game name and press enter to start.

the object will the defender the ball and the rim an net. The defender is the person trying to stop you from 
scoring. The ball is the object your shooting with and the rim an net are the object to make sure that the ball goes in.

The control are the arrow an WASD and the space bar keybind the WASD and the arrow are for you to move.
The space bar keybind is for you to shot the ball.

The enemy are the ones that stop you from scoring  they should behave like a defender arms up 
and blocking you path to the rim and your ball .

If you win you earn coins but if you lose you lose coin.
